Privacy Policy

Developer: Long Term Gaming
Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy describes the offline, free base version of Wonderwake: The Sleeping World, including version 0.0.127 (Android package com.longtermgaming.wonderwake).

Information handled by the game

Wonderwake stores puzzle progress, two save slots, and game settings locally on your device so you can continue playing. The game does not transmit this information to Long Term Gaming or third parties. No player account or sign-in is required, and we do not operate a cloud-save service for this version.

This version contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchase services. It does not collect or share personal information through the game or automatically send diagnostic or performance reports. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Optional vibration

The Android vibration permission provides optional haptic feedback during play. It does not collect personal information. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.

Support emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. Email providers process these communications as part of delivering and storing email. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We do not sell your support information or use it for advertising. Access is limited to people who need it to handle your request. We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the contact above; if information must be retained for a legal obligation, we will explain this.

Local retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you reset or remove it. The in-game erase option resets the selected journey. To remove all locally stored game data, use Android's app storage settings to clear Wonderwake's data, or uninstall the game. Device backup or restore behavior is controlled by your operating system and device settings. Long Term Gaming cannot recover deleted progress from a server.

Security and external services

Game saves use the device's app storage. Protecting access to your device helps protect this local data. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and is not published. No storage or communication system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google Play, your operating system, and any website hosting this policy operate separately from the game and may process information under their own privacy policies. Their operation does not mean Wonderwake itself transmits your saved progress or settings.

Audience

Wonderwake is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and older. The game does not collect personal information from players. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us to request its deletion.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy if our data practices change, including before introducing online features or purchase services that change those practices. The effective date above identifies this version. For privacy questions or support-data deletion requests, contact longtermgaming8@gmail.com.
